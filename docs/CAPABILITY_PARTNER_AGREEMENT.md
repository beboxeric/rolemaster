# Capability Partner Agreement / 能力伙伴合作协议

**Version:** 1.0 (DRAFT — pending legal review)
**Effective date:** to be set at execution
**Bilingual document.** Chinese version follows the English version below. In the event of conflict, the **English version controls** unless the Capability Partner is incorporated in the People's Republic of China, Hong Kong SAR, or Macau SAR, in which case the **Chinese version controls**.

---

## 1. Parties

This Capability Partner Agreement ("**Agreement**") is between:

- **RoleMaster** — the operator of `airolemaster.com` (legal entity to be inserted at execution); and
- **Capability Partner** — the entity registering and submitting AI products via `https://www.airolemaster.com/partners`, identified by the **Company Name** and **Contact** fields submitted at registration.

Each is a "Party"; together, the "Parties".

## 2. Scope

2.1 **Platform.** RoleMaster operates a B2B marketplace where AI products are packaged as job-skill bundles ("**RolePacks**") and made discoverable to enterprise customers via Solution Advisors.

2.2 **Capability Partner role.** The Capability Partner submits product materials, capability descriptions, role definitions, deployment notes, pricing, and supporting files (the "**Submission**") to the platform.

2.3 **Curation.** RoleMaster's curation team reviews each Submission, may request revisions, and decides whether to publish all or part of the Submission as live RolePacks. Curation decisions are at RoleMaster's discretion.

2.4 **Distribution.** Published RolePacks are made available to RoleMaster Solution Advisors who may recommend them to enterprise clients. Solution Advisor outreach to clients is governed by the separate Solution Advisor Agreement and is not within the scope of this Agreement.

## 3. Capability Partner obligations

3.1 The Capability Partner represents and warrants that:
  (a) it has full legal authority to submit the materials and license RoleMaster to use them for the purposes set out in this Agreement;
  (b) the Submission does not infringe any third-party intellectual-property right, trade secret, privacy right, or applicable law;
  (c) all factual claims in the Submission (capability descriptions, performance metrics, customer references, certifications) are accurate and not misleading;
  (d) the contact person identified at registration has authority to bind the Capability Partner to this Agreement.

3.2 The Capability Partner will keep the contact details (name, mobile, work email) current via the dashboard at `https://www.airolemaster.com/partners`.

3.3 If a published claim becomes inaccurate, the Capability Partner will notify RoleMaster within five (5) business days and submit a revised version.

## 4. Intellectual property

4.1 **Capability Partner IP.** All right, title, and interest in the underlying AI product, source code, models, weights, and proprietary methods remain solely with the Capability Partner.

4.2 **Limited license to RoleMaster.** The Capability Partner grants RoleMaster a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license, for the term of this Agreement plus a 90-day tail period, to:
  (a) use, reproduce, translate, summarize, and display the Submission on the RoleMaster platform;
  (b) generate derivative descriptive materials (capability summaries, role bundle descriptions, sales decks, FAQs) for the purpose of presenting the product to potential enterprise clients;
  (c) sublicense the right to view the published RolePack content to RoleMaster Solution Advisors and to enterprise clients evaluating RoleMaster offerings.

4.3 **AI-generated derivatives.** Materials produced by RoleMaster's AI Copilot from the Submission (e.g. capability extraction, role matching, sales-ready content) are owned jointly: the Capability Partner may use them externally for its own marketing, and RoleMaster may use them within the platform.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each Party will treat as confidential any non-public information designated as such, or which a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Confidential information may not be disclosed to third parties (other than employees, contractors, and platform users on a need-to-know basis) for three (3) years after disclosure.

5.2 Materials marked or clearly intended for public publication (RolePack listings, capability descriptions submitted for publication) are not treated as confidential after they are published in the catalog.

5.3 Each Party may disclose confidential information when compelled by law, provided the disclosing Party gives prompt notice (where lawful) and reasonable cooperation in seeking protective orders.

## 6. Data handling

6.1 RoleMaster stores the Submission and supporting files in Cloudflare D1 (database) and R2 (file storage), located in regions configured at the platform level. Storage region details are available on request.

6.2 The Capability Partner will not upload personal data of third parties (end-customer PII, employee personal data) without first anonymizing or pseudonymizing it.

6.3 Either Party may request export or deletion of submitted materials. Export will be provided as a JSON archive within ten (10) business days. Deletion of unpublished Submissions will be completed within thirty (30) days; published RolePacks may be unpublished within thirty (30) days but historical sales-deck PDFs already downloaded by Solution Advisors may persist outside the platform.

## 7. Fees and revenue share

7.1 Listing on RoleMaster is **free of charge** during the platform's beta period. Pricing terms for the post-beta period will be agreed in a written amendment before they take effect.

7.2 If a Solution Advisor closes a deal sourced through a published RolePack, the revenue-share percentage and payment terms will be governed by a separate Solution Advisor commercial agreement; the Capability Partner is invoiced only after the deal is closed and paid by the enterprise client.

## 8. Term and termination

8.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins when the Capability Partner clicks "I agree" during registration (or, if first registered before this Agreement existed, the first time it logs in after the Agreement is published) and continues until terminated.

8.2 **Termination for convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice (email to the contact address on file is sufficient).

8.3 **Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate immediately upon material breach by the other Party that is not cured within ten (10) business days of written notice.

8.4 **Effect of termination.** On termination:
  (a) the Capability Partner's account is deactivated within five (5) business days;
  (b) published RolePacks are unpublished from the catalog;
  (c) the IP license in §4.2 expires after the 90-day tail period;
  (d) accrued payment obligations survive.

## 9. Limitation of liability

9.1 To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party will be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages.

9.2 Each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at the greater of (i) the fees paid by the Capability Partner to RoleMaster in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, or (ii) USD 1,000.

9.3 The cap in §9.2 does not apply to: (a) breaches of confidentiality (§5); (b) IP indemnity (§4.1 warranties); (c) fraud or willful misconduct.

## 10. Governing law and disputes

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of **Hong Kong SAR**.

10.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement that cannot be resolved by good-faith negotiation within thirty (30) days will be referred to the **Hong Kong International Arbitration Centre (HKIAC)** for final and binding arbitration under the HKIAC Administered Arbitration Rules in force at the time. Seat: Hong Kong. Language: English (with Chinese translation available on request).

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement supersedes any prior understandings between the Parties on the subject matter.

11.2 **Amendments.** RoleMaster may publish updated versions of this Agreement on the platform. Material changes will be notified by email at least thirty (30) days before they take effect; continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

11.3 **Notices.** Email notices to the contact address on file (or, for RoleMaster, to `legal@rolemaster.io`) are valid written notice.

11.4 **No agency.** Nothing in this Agreement creates any agency, partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship between the Parties.

11.5 **Severability.** If any provision is found unenforceable, the rest of the Agreement remains in effect.

# 能力伙伴合作协议(中文版)

**版本:** 1.0(草案 — 待法务审核)
**生效日:** 待签署时填写
**双语版本。** 中文版本在以下情形下具有效力优先级:能力伙伴在中国大陆、香港特别行政区或澳门特别行政区注册的;其他情形以英文版本为准。

## 1. 双方

本《能力伙伴合作协议》("**本协议**")由以下双方签订:

- **RoleMaster** —— `airolemaster.com` 的运营方(具体法人主体待签署时确认);以及
- **能力伙伴** —— 通过 `https://www.airolemaster.com/partners` 注册并提交 AI 产品的实体,以注册时填写的"公司名称"和"联系人"识别。

## 2. 适用范围

2.1 **平台。** RoleMaster 运营一个 B2B 平台,将 AI 产品打包成"岗位能力包"("**RolePack**"),通过方案顾问推荐给企业客户。

2.2 **能力伙伴角色。** 能力伙伴向平台提交产品资料、能力描述、岗位定义、部署说明、价格和支撑材料(统称"**提交内容**")。

2.3 **策展。** RoleMaster 策展团队审阅每一份提交内容,可能要求修改,并决定是否将其全部或部分发布为正式 RolePack。策展决定由 RoleMaster 自行裁量。

2.4 **分发。** 已发布的 RolePack 提供给 RoleMaster 方案顾问,后者可向企业客户推荐。方案顾问与企业客户之间的对接由独立的《方案顾问协议》约定,不在本协议范围内。

## 3. 能力伙伴义务

3.1 能力伙伴声明并保证:
  (a)拥有完整法律权限提交相关材料并向 RoleMaster 授权按本协议使用;
  (b)提交内容不侵犯任何第三方知识产权、商业秘密、隐私权或适用法律;
  (c)提交内容中的事实陈述(能力描述、性能指标、客户案例、资质认证)真实、不误导;
  (d)注册时填写的联系人有权代表能力伙伴签订本协议。

3.2 能力伙伴应在 `https://www.airolemaster.com/partners` 上保持联系人信息(姓名、手机、工作邮箱)的更新。

3.3 已发布内容如出现不准确情形,能力伙伴应在 5 个工作日内通知 RoleMaster 并提交修订版。

## 4. 知识产权

4.1 **能力伙伴 IP。** AI 产品本身、源代码、模型、权重和专有方法的全部权利保留在能力伙伴。

4.2 **对 RoleMaster 的有限授权。** 能力伙伴授予 RoleMaster 全球、非独占、免特许使用费的许可,在本协议有效期内及终止后 90 天的过渡期内:
  (a)在 RoleMaster 平台上使用、复制、翻译、概括和展示提交内容;
  (b)生成衍生介绍材料(能力摘要、岗位描述、销售幻灯片、常见问题)用于向潜在企业客户介绍产品;
  (c)向 RoleMaster 方案顾问及评估中的企业客户授予查看已发布 RolePack 内容的次级许可。

4.3 **AI 生成的衍生作品。** RoleMaster AI Copilot 从提交内容生成的材料(能力梳理、岗位匹配、销售素材)归双方共有:能力伙伴可在外部用于自家市场推广,RoleMaster 可在平台内使用。

## 5. 保密

5.1 双方应将明确标注或合理理解为保密的非公开信息视为机密,在披露后 3 年内不得向第三方披露(出于必要的员工、外包方、平台用户除外)。

5.2 已发布或明显面向公开发布的内容(已上架 RolePack)在发布后不再视为机密。

5.3 任一方在被法律强制要求披露时可披露保密信息,但应在合法范围内尽快通知对方并配合争取保护令。

## 6. 数据处理

6.1 RoleMaster 将提交内容和上传文件存储于 Cloudflare D1(数据库)和 R2(对象存储),所在区域按平台配置;具体存储地区可索取详情。

6.2 能力伙伴不得在未匿名化或假名化的情况下上传第三方个人数据(终端客户 PII、员工个人数据)。

6.3 任一方可申请导出或删除已提交材料。导出以 JSON 归档形式在 10 个工作日内提供;未发布的提交内容删除将在 30 天内完成;已发布的 RolePack 可在 30 天内下架,但已下载至外部的销售幻灯片 PDF 可能继续在平台外存在。

## 7. 费用与分成

7.1 在平台 Beta 测试期间,RoleMaster 上架**免费**。Beta 后续期间的定价条款须经书面修订协议确认后才生效。

7.2 方案顾问通过已发布的 RolePack 成单时,具体分成比例与付款条款由独立的《方案顾问商业协议》约定;能力伙伴只在企业客户付款后才被结算。

## 8. 期限与终止

8.1 **期限。** 本协议自能力伙伴在注册时点击"同意"之时(对早于本协议生效前已注册的能力伙伴,自本协议公布后的首次登录之时)开始生效,持续至终止。

8.2 **任意终止。** 任一方可提前 30 天书面通知(发送邮件至档案中的联系邮箱即可)终止本协议。

8.3 **过错终止。** 一方有重大违约且未能在收到书面通知后 10 个工作日内修正,另一方可立即终止。

8.4 **终止后果。** 终止后:
  (a)能力伙伴账号在 5 个工作日内停用;
  (b)已发布 RolePack 从目录中下架;
  (c)第 4.2 条 IP 许可在 90 天过渡期后失效;
  (d)已产生的付款义务继续有效。

## 9. 责任限制

9.1 在法律允许的最大范围内,任一方都不对间接、附带、衍生或惩罚性损失承担责任。

9.2 各方在本协议下的累计责任上限为(i)能力伙伴在前 12 个月向 RoleMaster 支付的费用,或(ii)1,000 美元,以较高者为准。

9.3 第 9.2 条上限不适用于:(a)违反保密条款(§5);(b)知识产权赔偿(§4.1 声明保证);(c)欺诈或故意不当行为。

## 10. 适用法律与争议解决

10.1 本协议适用**香港特别行政区法律**。

10.2 因本协议产生的争议如无法在 30 天内通过善意协商解决,应提交**香港国际仲裁中心(HKIAC)**按照其届时有效的《机构仲裁规则》进行最终具有约束力的仲裁。仲裁地:香港。仲裁语言:英文(可申请中文翻译)。

## 11. 其他

11.1 **完整协议。** 本协议在标的事项上取代之前任何理解。

11.2 **修订。** RoleMaster 可在平台上发布更新版本。重大变更将至少提前 30 天以邮件通知;在生效日后继续使用即视为接受。

11.3 **通知。** 发送至档案联系邮箱(或对 RoleMaster 而言为 `legal@rolemaster.io`)的电子邮件构成有效书面通知。

11.4 **非代理关系。** 本协议不构成任何代理、合伙、合资或雇佣关系。

11.5 **可分性。** 任何条款被认定不可执行的,其他条款仍然有效。
